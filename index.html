Interactive demos

Simulations for teaching statistics and social-science methods. Each one runs in the browser, with nothing to install.

  • How many marbles are enough?

    Draw repeated samples from a bowl of red and blue marbles. The spread of the estimates depends on the size of the sample and hardly at all on the size of the bowl.

    Sampling · Margin of error

  • Comparing like with like

    The respondents who saw a campaign ad are older and more extreme than those who did not. Match them three ways and see which comparison recovers the effect of the ad.

    Matching · Causal inference

  • PCA playground

    Simulate data from a covariance matrix of your choosing and watch its structure surface in the eigenvalues, the scree plot, and the biplot.

    Principal components

  • Manifold learning playground

    Images of a clock hand at every angle lie on a circle in pixel space. Seven methods, from PCA to UMAP, try to recover that circle.

    Dimensionality reduction
