Multilevel modeling

Life satisfaction for 1,000 simulated respondents in four countries, built from the World Values Survey. With age as the predictor, a single regression gets the sign wrong, and one slope fits every country. With freedom of choice, the slope itself differs from country to country. The tests below tell the two cases apart.

The R script that simulated the data

The respondents are simulated. For each country, the script under the R code draws age, sex, and freedom of choice from that country's distributions in the seventh wave of the World Values Survey (Haerpfer et al. 2024), education from a regression on age and sex, and life satisfaction from a regression fitted to the country's full sample, rounded to the 1 to 10 scale. No real respondent appears in the data.
