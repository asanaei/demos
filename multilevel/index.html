Multilevel modeling

Life satisfaction for 1,000 simulated respondents in four countries, built from the World Values Survey. With age, one regression for everyone gets the sign wrong; with freedom of choice, the slope itself differs from country to country.

The R script that simulated the data

The respondents are simulated. The script under the R code draws age, gender, freedom of choice, and education from summaries of each country's sample in the seventh wave of the World Values Survey (Haerpfer et al. 2024). Life satisfaction comes from a latent score whose thresholds reproduce each country's real shares of answers 1 to 10, with slopes calibrated to the real ones, except that age has the same slope in every country, as the real data allow. No real respondent appears in the data.
